# Asserted Historical Dates Part

- 🔑 `it.vedph.asserted-historical-dates`
- ⚙️ [model](https://github.com/vedph/cadmus-core/blob/master/docs/models/general/asserted-historical-dates.md)
- 🎯 One or more [dates](../bricks/historical-date.md) with optional [assertions](../bricks/assertion.md) about their certainty level.

This part contains a list of [dates](../bricks/historical-date.md) with optional [assertions](../bricks/assertion.md); as such, for each date it just wraps a historical date brick and an assertion brick.

This part is used when you want to assign one or more dates (with different or equal probability levels) to the container item as a whole.
